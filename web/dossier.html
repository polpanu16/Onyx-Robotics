Introduction

ONYX-01 is a fictional transforming robot concept with dark armor and pale blue lighting. It explores the relationship between visual design and intelligent movement. The concept is created for an educational prompt-engineering project and does not describe a real commercial product.

Abstract

ONYX-01 connects AI-generated visual design, mathematical modeling, control-flow diagrams, and digital presentation in a single educational prototype. All specifications are fictional assumptions. No physical robot or measured performance is claimed.

01 / Visual design

Layered matte obsidian armor, graphite mechanical surfaces, a pointed helmet, narrow white-blue eyes, a V-shaped chest, and shoulder wheels form its identity. A single reference image anchors subsequent images. Generated details are visual concepts rather than manufacturing drawings.

04 / Control flow

Read sensors, then check battery. Below 20%, stop the mission and request charging. Otherwise check for obstacles. An obstacle triggers a stop and replanning before another sensor reading. A clear path permits movement, followed by another reading.

05 / Limitations

The model ignores terrain, payload, temperature, changing efficiency, and transient loads. No physical transformation mechanism has been validated. AI imagery can contain inconsistent geometry. This is an educational concept, not a certified controller or a commercial product.
